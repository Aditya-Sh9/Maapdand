Built to flag, not to clear

When the text cannot settle a rule, Maapdand says Cannot determine instead of guessing a pass. A missed violation costs more than a second look.

  • {% icon "scale" 20 %}It does not measure letter height or panel area. Those checks return Cannot determine until a person looks at the pack.
  • {% icon "book-open" 20 %}It covers the Packaged Commodities Rules only, not FSSAI, drug or other commodity-specific rules.
  • {% icon "pencil" 20 %}OCR misreads. Every value is shown to you for correction before the checks run.
  • {% icon "info" 20 %}It is not a legal notice. The final decision rests with the Legal Metrology officer.
